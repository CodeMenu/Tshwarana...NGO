    • Our Programs

    We at TSHWARANA are dedicated to providing numerous programs for the youth of South Africa in the field of STEM. Our goal is to help them acquire the necessary qualifications and experience to get into their desired careers. Our programs include coding classes, robotics workshops, and science camps. We believe that by providing these programs, we can help build a better future for our country.

    Tech-day

    Tech Day at Tswarahana is a dynamic event where innovation meets education. Geared towards high school students and the local community, this immersive experience introduces attendees to cutting-edge technology, gadgets, and skills.







    Expo-day

    Main purpose of our expo day is to promote our organization to the community, companies and schools. Create a network with other organization and a network with IT companies or any other companies to get involved by funding our program by (donating funds or donating equipment) including (intership/learnership/apprenticeship) Expo day will happen every two month, learners under our program will practically show off what they have learned from our program by proposing a project on the skills that they have obtained.Expo day basically will be a show and tell so schools and the locally community can understand what they will gain from our program also to uplift them to join us program.





    Skill Training Courses

    The courses that Tshwarana will provide will be basic skills training courses on hardware and software skills.
