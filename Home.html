    Vision

    Our vision is to help, empower and guide people to make aspirational choices about career choices , higher educational and skilled development learning.Our main focus is to provide informational technology (IT) skills training to empower the unskilled to be skilled.

    Mission

    Our mission at Tshwarana is to ignite the sparks of innovation and empowerment within every individual, especially the youth from underserved communities, by providing access to comprehensive IT education and guidance.Through our commitment to bridging the digital divide, we strive to equip young minds with the essential skills and resources necessary to thrive in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.With a focus on empowerment, education, and impact, we aim to inspire the next generation of leaders, innovators, and changemakers, fostering a brighter future for all.

    What Do We Do

    At Tshwarana,were passionate about empowring youth and communities through comprehensive IT education. We bridge the digital divide,providing essential skills and guidance to unlock opportunities and faster innovation in the digital age. Join us as we inspire,educate,and empower the leaders of tomorrow.
